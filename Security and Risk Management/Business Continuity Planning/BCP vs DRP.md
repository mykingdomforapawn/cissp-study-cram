Business Continuity Planning (BCP) and Disaster Recovery Planning (DRP) are two perspectives on the same problem — keeping the organization running through a disruption — rather than two separate disciplines. BCP is the strategic, organization-wide view: keeping critical business functions operating (or restoring them quickly) across people, facilities, and processes, not just systems. DRP is the technical view nested inside it: recovering the IT infrastructure, applications, and data that those business functions depend on.

In practice, DRP executes within the scope BCP defines. BCP decides which business functions matter most and how much downtime/data loss is tolerable; DRP builds the technical recovery procedures to hit those targets. The line between them isn't worth getting stuck on for the exam — what matters is recognizing which lens a question is using.
